## POST-RELEASE: GBA green ramp + palette search box removed (DONE, runtime-verified)
**GBA colour ramp.** GBA1-GBA10 shipped with six identical greens (GBA4 through GBA10 all `#38A169`, and GBA2 duplicated it too) - harmless in the old code list, but a visible defect once the colour-led palette put all ten on screen at once. Replaced with a perceptually even ten-step ramp generated in CIE LCh by `scripts/apply-gba-ramp.cjs` (idempotent, matches on `code: 'GBAn'` and rewrites only that record's `color` and `fill`; every other bundle byte untouched). Ramp: `#024F39 #065F40 #017142 #238247 #42924D #5DA254 #77B25B #92C263 #ADD26B #C9E274`.
Method and why it is not an HSL ramp: a straight HSL lightness sweep bunches badly through the greens - the first attempt scored a min adjacent dE of 3.8, with GBA6/GBA7 effectively identical. Building in LCh with an even L* climb (29->86), a hue drift blue-green->yellow-green (166->116 deg, because ten steps of ONE hue are not tellable apart at 5 px swatch size) and chroma 44->56 gives **min adjacent dE 8.0**. Parameters came from a small grid search maximising the smallest adjacent step subject to: every step in sRGB gamut, and >= 14 dE from every other area code. Nearest neighbour is LAND `#68D391` at 21.1.
Two traps worth recording: (1) the first search over-fit the metric - unbounded, it produced a near-black `#023328` and a YELLOW `#FCEA6E` that no longer read as building-area greens, because I had left the P/P ochres (OFP/SFP/EFP/PAT/DK/BAL) out of the collision set. Adding them and bounding L*/hue to keep the ramp green fixed it. (2) The darkest step trades against separation: L0=26 gives min dE 8.3 but only 1.55 contrast against the `#1e1e1e` canvas; L0=29 gives 8.0 and 1.73. Took L0=29 - the swatch is decorative and the tile also carries its code, but the dark end is still the weakest link if it ever needs revisiting.
**Palette search box removed** (`#area-palette-search`) at owner request; the group chips are the only filter now and cover the same ground in one click. `paletteTerm` and the term-matching branch of `paintPalette()` deleted with it, the palette signature no longer carries a term, and the empty state simplifies to "No types in this group." The palette gains ~30 px - at 1500x1050 it now shows ~22 tiles.
Gate impact: `dragTile` in the verifier had been typing into that box to park a tile at a stable position before measuring its box. It now clicks the code's GROUP CHIP instead, which also guarantees the dragged tile is on screen without scrolling (the earlier scroll-vs-scroll-restore race is gone for good). New assertions: no `#area-palette-search` in the panel, GBA still has ten floors, and **all ten GBA colours are distinct** - that last one fails against the pre-ramp bundle, so it is a real guard.
